# 6. Release Recommendation

**Would you release this build?**  
**No.**

This build should not be released. The single defect that most strongly drives that decision is **BUG-026 — Unit price on the cart line is editable by the user**. While the application contains many serious problems (payment-method radio buttons that allow both options to stay selected, terms checkbox that does not gate order placement, missing field validation, out-of-stock items that can still be added, uncapped discounts, incorrect VAT and grand-total calculations, and several security exposures around card data and authentication), most of those either block a happy path or produce an obvious failure the customer or the payment gateway would eventually notice. BUG-026 is different: it lets any signed-in user simply change the unit price of an item already in the cart and have that altered price accepted by the system. That turns a pricing engine into an honour system. A customer can reduce a ৳5,499 item to ৳1 and still reach a successful order confirmation. No amount of downstream validation on card length, CVV format, or coupon caps can compensate for a price that the client itself is allowed to rewrite. The other Critical defects (full card-number exposure, login that succeeds with an empty email, ability to place an order without accepting terms, negative or wildly incorrect grand totals) are also release blockers, yet they are either detectable at the payment gateway, limited to specific flows, or produce visibly broken numbers. An editable unit price is silent, client-controlled, and directly multiplies every other pricing and total calculation that follows it. Until that single control is removed and prices are treated as server-authoritative, the build cannot be considered safe for real customers or real money.
